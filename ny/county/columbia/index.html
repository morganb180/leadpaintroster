Hiring in Columbia County: quick guide

  1. Check the firm or person in the official list (US Environmental Protection Agency). Ours was retrieved 2026-10-12, so confirm the certification is still current.
  2. Ask for the certification or licence number in writing, plus the individual inspector's own credential.
  3. Decide what you need first: an inspection or a risk assessment.
  4. For abatement, use a certified abatement contractor, and have someone independent do the clearance test.
