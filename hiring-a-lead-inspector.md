# How to hire a lead inspector

Before you book a lead paint inspection, confirm the firm's certification and the inspector's own credential, agree which test you need, and ask what the written report will cover. Every listing on Lead Paint Roster shows its licence numbers and links the official source list.

## What should I ask a lead inspector before hiring?

* What is your firm certification number, and who will do the inspection (their individual credential)?
* Will you do a full inspection (surface by surface), a risk assessment, or both?
* Will you use an XRF analyzer, paint chip samples, or dust wipes?
* Will the report meet the requirements of the local rental-inspection or lead law?
* How soon will I get the written report and any lead-safe certificate?
* If hazards are found, can you do clearance testing after the repairs?
* Are you independent of the abatement contractor?
