Before you hire

You cannot tell by looking.

EPA says the only way to know whether paint contains lead is a test by a certified inspector or risk assessor. Paint in good condition is usually not a hazard.

What lead paint is, and when it is a hazard →

A risk assessment looks for hazards.

An inspection maps where lead-based paint is. A risk assessment looks for deteriorated paint and lead in dust or soil, and recommends how to control it.

Inspection vs risk assessment →

Abatement is a different licence from repair work.

Lead abatement is work meant to permanently eliminate a lead hazard. An RRP renovator certification is not an abatement licence.

RRP vs lead abatement →
