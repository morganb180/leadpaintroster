Our records hold 1,952 lead paint listings: 1,636 New York firms certified by the US EPA, 256 New Jersey firms certified by NJ DCA, and 60 Massachusetts listings from the state's licensed lead inspector list. 1,934 are firms and 18 are individual inspectors (all in Massachusetts). The five New York City counties account for 888 listings.

[Download the county table (CSV)](https://leadpaintroster.com/lead-paint-licence-data/lead-paint-licensees-by-county.csv) · listing-level data: [api/firms.json](https://leadpaintroster.com/api/firms.json)

## How we count

- **One row per listing.** A listing is a firm, or in Massachusetts an individual inspector we could not group into a firm. New Jersey firms on both the DCA evaluation and abatement lists are merged into one listing.
- **Firm vs individual.** New York (EPA) and New Jersey (DCA) lists certify firms. The Massachusetts list licenses individual inspectors. We grouped them into firms by business email domain or company name, and 18 could not be grouped.
- **County.** Taken from the listing's address (ZIP or city). Listings with an address outside the state that licenses them are counted on a separate row.
- **Services.** A listing can offer more than one service, so service columns do not add up to the listing total.
- **Not included:** NJDOH individual licensees, EPA-certified individuals in New York and the Massachusetts deleading contractor list (see [about our data](https://leadpaintroster.com/about-our-data/)).

## Frequently asked questions

**How many certified lead paint firms are in New York?**

Our records list 1,636 New York firms certified by the US EPA for lead-based paint evaluation or abatement, from the EPA firm locator retrieved 2026-10-12.

**Does this count every licensed lead inspector?**

No. It counts listings in our directory. New Jersey and New York lists certify firms, not individual workers, and some lists (NJDOH individuals, EPA individuals in New York, the MA deleading list) are not included yet.

**Why are some Massachusetts listings individuals?**

Massachusetts licenses individual private lead inspectors. We group them into firms by business email domain or company name; 18 could not be grouped.

**Can I download the data?**

Yes. The county table is a CSV on this page, and every listing is in api/firms.json.
