How much do lead paint inspection and abatement cost?

A national HomeAdvisor guide puts a lead paint inspection at $239-$437 (about $338 on average) and lead paint removal at $1,478-$5,520 (about $3,499).

ItemTypical range (national)
Lead paint inspection$239-$437, average about $338 (HomeAdvisor lead paint inspection cost guide)
Lead paint removal / abatement$1,478-$5,520, average about $3,499 (HomeAdvisor lead paint removal cost guide)

National figures, not local quotes. Large homes and multi-unit buildings cost more. Try the estimate tool.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a lead paint inspection cost?

A national HomeAdvisor guide puts a lead paint inspection at $239-$437, about $338 on average. This is a national figure, not a local quote.

How much does lead paint removal cost?

HomeAdvisor's national lead paint removal guide puts removal / abatement at $1,478-$5,520, about $3,499 on average. Large homes and multi-unit buildings cost more.

Does the estimate include clearance testing?

Only if you tick it. The estimate tool treats a clearance re-test after work as a second inspection at the same national range, which is an assumption, not a quoted price.
