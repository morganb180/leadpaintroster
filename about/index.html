About Lead Paint Roster

Lead Paint Roster is a free directory of certified lead-based paint inspectors, risk assessors and abatement firms in New Jersey, New York and Massachusetts, built from NJ DCA, US EPA and Massachusetts licence records.

Lead Paint Roster is for landlords, tenants, buyers and parents who need a certified lead inspector, risk assessor or abatement contractor.

Where the information comes from

  • New Jersey: New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (official list)
  • New York: US Environmental Protection Agency (official list)
  • Massachusetts: Commonwealth of Massachusetts (licensed private lead inspector list) (official list)

1,952 listings, grouped by state, county and city. Guides: Lead inspection vs risk assessment, Lead inspection & abatement cost + estimate tool, Landlord lead rules: NJ, NYC, MA, How to hire a lead inspector.

How it's built

  • Listings are rebuilt from the official source files on each refresh; every listing shows its source and retrieval date.
  • Rules and cost guides link the statute, regulation, agency page or official fee schedule they rely on. Where we couldn't confirm something from an official source, we say so or leave it out.
  • Nothing here is legal advice. Requirements change, so confirm with the agency or town before you act.

Independence

Lead Paint Roster is independent. It isn't affiliated with any government agency, licensing board or contractor, and a listing is not an endorsement. See how we make money, how we verify listings and about our data.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public certification records: NJ Department of Community Affairs certified lead evaluation and abatement contractor lists (revised Aug 11 and July 15, 2026), the US EPA Lead-Based Paint Professional Locator (New York firms) and the Massachusetts licensed private lead inspector list, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP/city-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Lead Paint Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
