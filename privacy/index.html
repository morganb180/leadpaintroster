Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This policy covers Lead Roster (leadpaintroster.com, "we").

What we collect

  • Listing requests and messages: the listing request form opens your own email app; we receive only what you send us by email, and use it to verify and process the request.
  • Server logs: our host keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't run third-party trackers.

Business listings

Listings come from public licensing or certification records (see About our data). Professionals can claim, correct or ask to remove their listing at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't share your messages except with service providers that host the site and deliver email.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to delete it, email hello@leadpaintroster.com.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date above.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public certification records: NJ Department of Community Affairs certified lead evaluation and abatement contractor lists (revised Aug 11 and July 15, 2026), the US EPA Lead-Based Paint Professional Locator (New York firms) and the Massachusetts licensed private lead inspector list, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP/city-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Lead Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
