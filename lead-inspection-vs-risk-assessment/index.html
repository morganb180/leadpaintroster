What is the difference between a lead inspection and a risk assessment?

A lead-based paint inspection finds out, surface by surface, whether a home has lead-based paint and where it is. A risk assessment looks for lead hazards, such as deteriorated paint and lead in dust or soil, and recommends ways to control them. Many rental laws ask for one or the other, so check which you need. EPA explains how to find qualified professionals (EPA: finding a qualified professional).

How much do lead paint inspection and abatement cost?

A national HomeAdvisor guide puts a lead paint inspection at $239-$437 (about $338 on average) and lead paint removal at $1,478-$5,520 (about $3,499).
