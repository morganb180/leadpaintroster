# Lead paint inspection and abatement cost

Reviewed by Lead Paint Roster staff

## How much do lead paint inspection and abatement cost?

A national HomeAdvisor guide puts a lead paint inspection at $239-$437 (about $338 on average) and lead paint removal at $1,478-$5,520 (about $3,499).

| Item | Typical range (national) |
| --- | --- |
| Lead paint inspection | $239-$437, average about $338 ([HomeAdvisor lead paint inspection cost guide](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/environmental-safety/test-or-remove-toxic-lead/)) |
| Lead paint removal / abatement | $1,478-$5,520, average about $3,499 ([HomeAdvisor lead paint removal cost guide](https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/environmental-safety/remove-toxic-lead/)) |

National figures, not local quotes. Large homes and multi-unit buildings cost more. Try the [estimate tool](https://leadpaintroster.com/lead-inspection-cost/).
