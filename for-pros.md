# For professionals

Every current licensee on the NJ DCA, EPA, MA lists gets a free listing on Lead Paint Roster, built from the official record and linked to it.

## Free listing

* Shown on your county and city pages, ordered by location and name.
* Shows your license numbers and a link to the state record, so homeowners can check you.
* [Claim it](https://leadpaintroster.com/claim-listing/?type=claim) to confirm the record or tell us a service area, or to fix an error. Phone numbers, email addresses and websites are not shown on the public listing. Homeowners reach you through the quote form.

## Certification badge

Add a free badge to your website that shows your certification and links to your listing: https://leadpaintroster.com/badges/

## Featured placement

Not available yet. When it is, featured spots will be labeled "Ad", limited per county, open only to current licensees, and shown alongside the full free list, never instead of it. To hear when it opens, send an [advertising request](https://leadpaintroster.com/claim-listing/?type=ads).
