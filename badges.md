Show clients your lead paint certification, and let them check it in one click. Each badge names the certification on the official list for your listing and links to your free Lead Paint Roster profile.

## Badge rules

- Only the US EPA, NJ DCA and MA DLS issue these credentials. Lead Paint Roster does not certify or endorse anyone and is not affiliated with any agency.
- Use only wording that matches your credential, and only while you are on the current official list.
- Free and optional; it does not change listing order.
