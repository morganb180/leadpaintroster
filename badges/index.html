Certification wording

The wording depends on who issued your credential: EPA-Certified for New York firms on the US EPA lead-based paint firm list, NJ DCA-Certified for New Jersey firms on the NJ DCA certified lead evaluation or abatement contractor lists, and MA DLS-Licensed for Massachusetts listings built from the MA DLS licensed lead inspector list.

How listing links work

  • Every listing has its own profile page: https://leadpaintroster.com/firms/<name>-<listing-id>/, for example https://leadpaintroster.com/firms/a-j-environmental-contractor-llc-ny0004/. Find yours in the list of all listed pros. The code generator above fills it in.
  • The listing ID is a state code followed by a number (for example NY0004, NJ0001 or MA0040).
  • Badge files are named /badges/<issuer>-<type>-<style>.svg. Issuers: epa, nj-dca, ma-dls. Types: lead-firm, evaluation, abatement, evaluation-abatement (EPA); lead-contractor, evaluation, abatement, evaluation-abatement (NJ DCA); inspector, risk-assessor (MA DLS). Styles: seal, light, dark, compact.

Badge rules

  • What it says: your listing appears on the official list (US EPA, NJ DCA or MA DLS) with the certification shown, as of the date on the badge. Clicking it takes people to your Lead Paint Roster listing, where they can check your certification details and the source list themselves.
  • Who certifies: only the US EPA, NJ DCA and MA DLS issue these credentials. Lead Paint Roster does not certify, license, inspect or endorse anyone. The badge is not issued, approved or endorsed by any agency, and Lead Paint Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency.
  • Pick accurate wording. Only use wording that matches your credential. The general wording (Lead-Based Paint Firm, Lead Contractor, Lead Inspector) fits any listing from that issuer. The code generator only offers wording that matches the official list for your listing.
  • Only while certified. Use the badge only while you are on the current official list. If your certification lapses, we remove your listing and you must remove the badge.
  • Free and optional. It does not change your listing order. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment.
  • Don't change the artwork or wording, combine it with government seals or logos, or describe it as "EPA-approved", "state-approved" or "Lead Paint Roster certified". You may scale it. Keep the seal at least 120 px wide.
