How we verify listings

Every listing comes from a state licensing agency's own public records. Nothing is added from reviews or scraped directories.

On this page

  1. Sources and last retrieval
  2. What we check
  3. What we don't verify
  4. Corrections

Sources and last retrieval

  • New Jersey: New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (source), retrieved 2026-10-12.
  • New York: US Environmental Protection Agency (source), retrieved 2026-10-12.
  • Massachusetts: Commonwealth of Massachusetts (licensed private lead inspector list) (source), retrieved 2026-10-12.

What we check

  • The licence appears on the agency record, and the licence number and expiry date (where published) are shown on the listing with a link to the source.
  • Records are grouped by firm or shared address; individuals without a published address are flagged.

What we don't verify

  • Workmanship, prices, insurance or availability.
  • Contact details a pro adds later, until the claim process checks them against the licence record.

Corrections

Use claim, correct or remove a listing. See also about our data.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public certification records: NJ Department of Community Affairs certified lead evaluation and abatement contractor lists (revised Aug 11 and July 15, 2026), the US EPA Lead-Based Paint Professional Locator (New York firms) and the Massachusetts licensed private lead inspector list, retrieved 2026-10-12.
