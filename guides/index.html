How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public NJ DCA, US EPA and Massachusetts licence records, retrieved and dated. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing is not an endorsement.

How they are ordered

By location and name. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and never reorders free listings.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the licence number and check it with NJ DCA, the EPA firm locator, or the Massachusetts list. The National Lead Information Center is 1-800-424-5323.

Licensed lead pro? Your free listing is already here, built from the official record. Claim it to confirm the record or tell us a service area. Phone, email and website are not shown on the listing.
