For professionals

Every current licensee on the NJ DCA, EPA, MA lists gets a free listing on Lead Paint Roster, built from the official record and linked to it.

Free listing

  • Shown on your county and city pages, ordered by location and name.
  • Shows your license numbers and a link to the state record, so homeowners can check you.
  • Claim it to add a phone number, website or service area, or to fix an error.

Certification badge

Add a free badge to your website that shows your EPA, NJ DCA or MA DLS certification and links to your listing. Get your badge code.

Featured placement

Not available yet. When it is, featured spots will be labeled "Ad", limited per county, open only to current licensees, and shown alongside the full free list, never instead of it. To hear when it opens, send an advertising request.

Disclaimer: Listings are compiled from public certification records: NJ Department of Community Affairs certified lead evaluation and abatement contractor lists (revised Aug 11 and July 15, 2026), the US EPA Lead-Based Paint Professional Locator (New York firms) and the Massachusetts licensed private lead inspector list, retrieved 2026-10-12. ZIP/city-to-county matching uses GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Inclusion is not an endorsement, and Lead Paint Roster is not affiliated with any licensing agency. Free listings are ordered by location and name, never by payment; any paid placement will be labeled "Ad". Always verify a licence with the issuing agency before hiring.
