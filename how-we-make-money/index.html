How we make money

Lead Paint Roster is free to use. Here is how it is, and will be, paid for, and the rules we hold ourselves to.

Today

  • Nothing is for sale. Every listing is free and built from official public records.
  • Listings are ordered by location and name. No one can pay to appear higher, to get a license badge, or to have a competitor removed.

Planned

  • Featured placements, clearly labeled "Ad", shown alongside, never instead of, the full free list.
  • Quote requests routed to certified inspectors near you, if we add them, will be free for property owners. Inspectors may pay a per-request fee; that will never change who is listed or the order of the free list.

Our rules

  • Paid or not, every listing must match a current official record.
  • Guides and cost pages are written independently of advertisers and cite their sources.
  • If we ever earn a referral fee from a link, the link will say so.

Professionals: see claim or correct your listing.

How this directory works

Where listings come from

Public NJ DCA, US EPA and Massachusetts licence records, retrieved and dated. Not affiliated with those agencies. A listing is not an endorsement.

How they are ordered

By location and name. Any paid placement is labeled "Ad" and never reorders free listings.

Verify before you hire

Ask for the licence number and check it with NJ DCA, the EPA firm locator, or the Massachusetts list. The National Lead Information Center is 1-800-424-5323.

Licensed lead pro? Your free listing is already here, built from the official record. Claim it to confirm the record or tell us a service area. Phone, email and website are not shown on the listing.
