Our advice

What should I check before hiring a lead pro in Massachusetts?

  1. Check the firm in the official Massachusetts DLS list and ask for its certification number in writing.
  2. Decide between an inspection and a risk assessment, then get at least two written quotes.
  3. After any abatement, use an independent clearance test.
