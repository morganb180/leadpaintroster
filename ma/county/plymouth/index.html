How do I hire a lead inspector in Plymouth County?

Check the firm in the official Massachusetts list, ask for the certification number in writing, decide between an inspection and a risk assessment, and use an independent clearance test after any abatement.

  1. Check the firm or person in the official list (Commonwealth of Massachusetts (licensed private lead inspector list)). Ours was retrieved 2026-10-12, so confirm the certification is still current.
  2. Ask for the certification or licence number in writing, plus the individual inspector's own credential.
  3. Decide what you need first: an inspection or a risk assessment.
  4. For abatement, use a certified abatement contractor, and have someone independent do the clearance test.
