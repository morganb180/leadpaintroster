# How to get quotes from certified lead inspectors

Lead Roster doesn't take quote requests online yet. The fastest route is to contact 2-3 licensed pros yourself: every listing shows the phone, email or website from the official record.

## Three steps

1. **Find your area.** Search above or [choose your state](https://leadpaintroster.com/). County and city pages list every licensee with an address there.
2. **Contact 2-3 pros.** Ask whether they serve your address, and when they can come out. For a rental or a child's elevated blood-lead result, say so up front: the inspection type (visual, dust wipe, full inspection or risk assessment) depends on why you need it.
3. **Compare written quotes.** Check each licence number against the state record (linked on every listing).

## Questions to ask

* What is your firm certification number, and who will do the inspection (their individual credential)?
* Will you do a full inspection (surface by surface), a risk assessment, or both?
* Will you use an XRF analyzer, paint chip samples, or dust wipes?
* Will the report meet the requirements of the local rental-inspection or lead law?
* How soon will I get the written report and any lead-safe certificate?
* If hazards are found, can you do clearance testing after the repairs?
* Are you independent of the abatement contractor?
