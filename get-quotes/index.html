Get free quotes from up to 3 certified lead pros

Tell us what you need and where. We send your request to up to 3 certified lead inspectors, risk assessors or abatement firms nearest you that are licensed for your state (NJ DCA, US EPA in New York, or Massachusetts), and email you their names. They contact you directly. It's free, and there's no obligation.

What do you need?

Not sure? We'll send it to certified inspectors / risk assessors, who can tell you which you need.

How it works

  1. We match your ZIP to firms licensed for your state that offer the service you chose, closest first. Firms can't pay to be picked.
  2. Each pro gets your name, contact details, ZIP, what you need and your note by email.
  3. You get an email listing the pros we contacted. We keep a copy of your request to run the service and never sell it. We only send you tips and offers if you tick the optional box.

Prefer to choose yourself?

Search your city, county or ZIP to see every licensee, with phone and licence numbers.

Questions to ask

  • What is your firm certification number, and who will do the inspection (their individual credential)?
  • Will you do a full inspection (surface by surface), a risk assessment, or both?
  • Will you use an XRF analyzer, paint chip samples, or dust wipes?
  • Will the report meet the requirements of the local rental-inspection or lead law?
  • How soon will I get the written report and any lead-safe certificate?
  • If hazards are found, can you do clearance testing after the repairs?
  • Are you independent of the abatement contractor?
